01 — What we do

Built around the player.

Three periods. One clear goal. We move European players from their home ice to the right North American program, navigating the complexities of AAA, Prep, Junior, and College to perfectly align your hockey and academic future.

01 / First Period

The Blueprint

We build a personalized, long-term roadmap tailored to your game and academics. With pathways constantly evolving, we ensure every decision maximizes your future options and keeps your development moving forward.

02 / Second Period

The Icetime

It's not just about playing; it's about playing where it counts. We guide you to the specific showcases, ID camps, and tournaments that give you the right ice time to prove your skills in front of the scouts and decision-makers that actually matter.

03 / Third Period

The Roster Spot

We don't chase the flashiest teams; we chase your growth. We find the specific AAA, Prep, or Junior roster spot that provides the ultimate environment for your long-term development.

02 — How it works

A path with no shortcuts.

No guesswork. No empty promises. Just a proven four-step process to take you from European ice to a North American roster.

01

Watch & Evaluate

An honest baseline of your game and academics. We evaluate your pace, compete level, and hockey IQ to project exactly how your style translates to North American ice.

  • Game Film & Live Scouting
  • Comprehensive Academic Evaluation
  • Honest NA Projection
02

Map the Path

A custom roadmap built for the modern game. We clearly explain the differences between AAA, Prep, USHL, and CHL to your family, helping you navigate complex rules while ensuring your hockey path perfectly aligns with your long-term educational goals.

  • League & Pathway Breakdown
  • Hockey & Academic Alignment
  • Custom timelines depending on age group
03

Develop with Intent

Preparation starts before you leave. We align your on-ice and off-ice habits with North American demands—adapting to smaller ice, faster pace, and a new culture.

  • NA Style & Tactical Prep
  • Off-Ice Readiness
  • Language & Cultural Transition
04

Place & Support

We read the fine print on every offer. Because your future relies on education just as much as hockey, we rigorously vet the academic options, billet families, and coaching to secure the ultimate fit.

  • Coach & Roster Vetting
  • Academic Fit & Contract Review
  • Year-Round NA Mentorship
