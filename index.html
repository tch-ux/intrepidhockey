01 — What we do

Built around the player.

Three disciplines, one mission — move European players from their home ice to AAA, Prep, CHL, USHL and Junior A in North America.

01 / Service

Scouting

European U15 — U20 evaluations across leagues and tournaments. Detailed reports built for AAA, Prep, CHL, USHL and Junior A pathways.

02 / Service

Advising

Career strategy for European players and families crossing to North America. League selection, school fit, commitments, contract reads.

03 / Service

Development

Year-round programs preparing European U15 — U20 players for the pace, structure and standards of NA hockey.

02 — How it works

A path with no shortcuts.

Four stages. Honest reads. The work shows up later — in the games, in the calls, in the offers.

01

Watch & evaluate

Live and video evaluation of European U15 — U20 players across full seasons — pace, decisions, compete level, transitions, projection vs NA standard.

  • Game film breakdown
  • In-arena scouting in Europe
  • Skating & hockey IQ scoring
02

Map the path

A custom NA roadmap — AAA, Prep, CHL, USHL or other Junior A. Honest reads on timing, fit and the realistic short list for the family.

  • Pathway analysis
  • School & league fit
  • 12-month timeline
03

Develop with intent

On-ice and off-ice work tied to what NA scouts and coaches actually look for. Closing the gap before the player arrives.

  • Skill modules
  • Strength & recovery
  • Cultural & language prep
04

Place & support

When the offer comes from the right program, we read the room. Coaches, billet families, paperwork — we sit on your side of the table.

  • Coach & program intros
  • Commitment review
  • Year-round mentorship in NA
