2. What Data We Collect

When you submit an evaluation request, we collect:

  • Player's first and last name
  • Player's birth year and playing position
  • Player's current team
  • Parent / guardian name
  • Contact email and phone number
  • Elite Prospects profile link (optional)
  • Goals and background information you provide

We do not collect sensitive personal data, financial information, or data from third-party trackers beyond standard website analytics.

3. Why We Collect It

We use this information solely to:

  • Evaluate your player's profile and prepare a personalized development overview
  • Contact you to discuss the evaluation and next steps
  • Respond to your inquiries

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Legal Basis

We process your data on the basis of your explicit consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)), given when you submit the evaluation form. You may withdraw consent at any time by emailing hello@intrepid.sk.

5. How Long We Keep It

We retain submitted information for as long as our consulting relationship is active, plus 12 months for follow-up purposes. After that, data is deleted unless we are legally required to retain it longer.

6. Your Rights Under GDPR

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Request restriction of processing
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with the Slovak Data Protection Authority (Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov SR)

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@intrepid.sk.

7. Data Security

Submitted information is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored securely. Access is limited to Intrepid Hockey personnel directly involved in evaluation.

8. Cookies and Analytics

This website uses only essential technical cookies required for basic site operation. No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies are placed on your device. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some site features may not function correctly as a result.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be communicated via the website.
